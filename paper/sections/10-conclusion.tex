\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We turned the \forget{} mutation from a syntactic test into a defeasible reasoning framework in which lapses of values, skills and derivations are defeaters, cues are prioritised exceptions, and decisions come with proofs. We characterised what the compiled theories conclude, showed that team defeat delivers per-hypothesis recovery, related the framework to the published semantics, kept decisions sound under bounded search, and obtained a polynomial decision for usability. The implementation agrees with the semantics on every check we ran, and its decisions change Tamarin verdicts. With adversarial cues, conflicts become two-sided and the choice of defeasible semantics becomes a modelling decision about how humans act in doubt; a translation guarantee and an implemented adversarial extension are the next steps.
