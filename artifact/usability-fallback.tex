\paragraph{Usability fallback.}
When $\Pibar=\varnothing$ and bounded enumeration truncates without a surviving derivation, the engine decides target usability by the restricted saturation of \cref{thm:poly} over $\Kacc(\sigma'')$. A positive result permits \preserve{} or acceptance of a replacement; a negative result completes only that target's usability check. Thus \skipm{} still requires an exhaustive candidate set and all candidates to be unusable. Saturation results are recorded separately from the enumerated DL traces. Enumeration remains necessary for replacement candidates, derivation traces and $\Pibar\neq\varnothing$; incomplete re-acquisition still yields \unknowncov{}.
